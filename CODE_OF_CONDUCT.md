# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on code and outcomes, not individuals.
- Assume positive intent and ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Welcome contributors across experience levels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, or intimidation.
- Deliberate disruption, doxxing, or sharing private information.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome conduct.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or block participants for violations.

## Reporting

For serious issues, contact maintainers privately via the security/support channel documented in `SECURITY.md`.
